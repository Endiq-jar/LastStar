# Weapon texture

`kickin_weapon_texture.png` is the image texture from **FREE Low Poly Weapon Pack** by Kickin' It Studios: https://kickin-it-studios.itch.io/low-poly-weapon-pack. The creator permits use and modification in games, but prohibits reselling the assets as 3D models; credit is appreciated. Retrieved from the pack copy at https://github.com/char1ieb0010110-oss/improved-fortnight/tree/75f672f835697e2c89685beae995c81caac23613/public/assets/items/weapons . The game's sword geometry is original and uses this palette texture; the pack's weapon models are not included.
