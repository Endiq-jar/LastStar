# Creatures

`Demon.gltf`, `BlueDemon.gltf`, and `Ghost_Skull.gltf` are animated game-ready creatures from Quaternius' **Ultimate Monsters Bundle**, CC0 1.0: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html .
Source copy: https://github.com/kxpedro/vp-game/tree/f7f686bced6750c59dddf3a3c2325a39e1c4f245/assets/enemies/quaternius_ultimate_monsters . The glTF files embed their textures and animation data. No MantisDA assets are included; its itch.io download was not accessible in this environment.
