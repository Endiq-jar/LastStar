# Creatures

`Demon.gltf`, `BlueDemon.gltf`, `Ghost_Skull.gltf`, and `Orc.gltf`, and `Dragon_Evolved.gltf`, and `Ninja.gltf` are animated game-ready creatures from Quaternius' **Ultimate Monsters Bundle**, CC0 1.0: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html .
Source copy: https://github.com/kxpedro/vp-game/tree/f7f686bced6750c59dddf3a3c2325a39e1c4f245/assets/enemies/quaternius_ultimate_monsters . The glTF files embed their textures and animation data. No MantisDA assets are included; its itch.io download was not accessible in this environment.


`Ghost_Skull_Scout.glb` and `Orc_Scout.glb` are simplified derivatives of the
above original CC0 models, used for Hollow scouts. Created with glTF Transform
4.2.1 `weld` then `simplify`: ghost ratio 0.48/error 0.02, orc ratio 0.25/error
0.03. Animations, skins and joint attributes are retained. The original files
remain here. These variants have not been visually assessed during animation.
