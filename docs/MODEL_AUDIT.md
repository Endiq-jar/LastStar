The new 20 interior props are made of two or three shared box meshes each (24–36 triangles per prop); they use flat materials rather than hand-painted textures or normal maps. Only two are placed in each house, and their geometry is hidden beyond 35 m. This is not a multi-level LOD system.

Settings now offer Ultra Low, Low, Medium, High and Ultra, controlling render scale, sun shadows, effects and vegetation density. Ultra Low starts at 60% render scale with sun shadows off; Low starts at 70%, Medium at 85%, and High/Ultra at 100%. Ultra Low, Low and Medium dynamically scale down when frames are slow. These settings do not impose triangle or texture budgets, nor provide texture-resolution variants. No device FPS or RAM budgets have been verified. Procedural characters, NPCs, weapons and other generated assets need a separate runtime mesh census; this report only reads glTF files. Further character/animation and building redesign, material/texture work, actual LODs and on-device profiling remain to be done. Environment assets have not been changed.
