# LAST STAR
### When the world loses its light, one star must remember.

**Genre:** 3D Open-World Action RPG  
**Platform:** Android (primary), with controller support  
**Engine:** Unity  
**Visual Style:** Anime-inspired 3D fantasy  
**Gameplay:** Real-time sword combat, spellcasting, exploration, dungeons, boss battles, quests, and character relationships.

## 1. Core Game Premise

The world of Elaris is inhabited by people who possess a mysterious supernatural potential known as a **Gift**.
Approximately 80% of the world's NPC population are Gifted, while every playable character possesses a Gift.
However, possessing a Gift does not mean someone knows how to use it.
To awaken and develop their abilities, Gifted individuals need a special magical book called a **Wander**.
A Wander is more than a spellbook. It is a personal record of magical knowledge that allows its owner to:

- Discover and learn spells.
- Create entirely new spells.
- Modify existing spells.
- Combine compatible magical abilities.
- Develop their own combat style.
- Record discoveries made during exploration.

Every Wander is different. Its appearance, magical structure, and potential depend on its owner.

At the beginning, players have only 3–4 basic spells. They must explore, experiment, fight, complete quests, and discover forgotten magical knowledge to expand their abilities.

## 2. The Three Foundations of Magic

### Gift
A Gift is an individual's unique magical potential.

Examples:
- Fire manipulation.
- Spatial distortion.
- Gravity control.
- Sound manipulation.
- Shadow manipulation.
- Time-related abilities.

Not every Gift is equally powerful. Some are excellent for combat, while others are designed for exploration, support, defense, or unusual applications.

### Wander
A Wander is the magical book used to develop and control a Gift.

It contains:
- Spell collection.
- Spell creation.
- Spell modification.
- Magical research.
- Spell combinations.
- Mastery records.

Players begin with a limited collection and gradually develop a unique spell library.

### Light
Light is the energy required to activate spells.

Every spell consumes Light depending on its complexity and level.

Light Capacity can be improved through:
- Character progression.
- Gift mastery.
- Special equipment.
- Rare materials.
- Story discoveries.
- Certain side quests.

Light regenerates naturally, but its regeneration rate depends on the character's development and equipment.

## 3. Spell Progression

The game should feature more than 100 spells across multiple magical categories.

Spells have individual mastery levels, with a maximum of Level 30.

Players should not simply collect spells. They should learn how each spell works and develop it through repeated use and experimentation.

Example:

**Ember Shot**
- Lv. 1: Fires a small fire projectile.
- Lv. 5: Increased projectile speed.
- Lv. 10: Explodes on impact.
- Lv. 15: Can pierce weak enemies.
- Lv. 20: Creates a burning area.
- Lv. 25: Unlocks a charged version.
- Lv. 30: Unlocks its advanced evolution.(this is jjst an example imaplement many spells but unlock only 2-4 spells at first they need to grinf to unlock)

Not every spell needs the same upgrades. Some should gain entirely new mechanics rather than just increased damage.

## 4. World Structure

**World Name:** Elaris

Elaris contains 14 major settlements, multiple explorable regions, ancient ruins, hidden locations, and dangerous Hollows.

Regions should connect through exploration and story progression instead of feeling like disconnected levels.

Each region should have:
- Many Unique enemies.
- Many Local factions.
- Main and side quests.
- Hidden treasures.
- Many Dungeons.
- Many Regional bosses.
- Many Unique materials.
- Environmental storytelling.

## 5. Important World Elements

**Hollows:** Mysterious openings leading into dangerous and often distorted locations.

**Hollow Creatures:** Monsters that originate from or have been corrupted by Hollows.

**Star Guard:** An organization responsible for protecting settlements and investigating supernatural threats.

**Seven Seals:** Ancient structures that maintain the barriers protecting Elaris.

**The First Silence:** An ancient catastrophe that changed the history of the world.

**Memory Vault:** An ancient structure containing lost memories, forbidden knowledge, and fragments of forgotten history.

**The Keeper:** A mysterious title associated with the primary antagonist.

**Remnants:** Mysterious traces of the ancient catastrophe that still influence the present world.

## 7. Design Philosophy

Last Star should reward curiosity, experimentation, preparation, and skill.
Not every enemy should be easy to defeat. Some bosses should require players to understand attack patterns, prepare suitable spells, upgrade equipment, and use the environment.
Winning should feel earned.
The world should also have meaningful discoveries that are not marked directly on the map. Players should sometimes discover hidden spells, secret dungeons, unusual characters, and forgotten stories by exploring on their own.

Combat system
Combat should combine swordplay, magic, dodging, blocking, and enemy pattern recognition.

Suggested combat mechanics:
- Light and heavy sword attacks.
- Charged attacks.
- Perfect dodge.
- Parrying.
- Spell casting while moving.
- Spell combinations.
- Aerial combat.
- Enemy stagger.
- Boss weak points.
- Special finishing attacks.
Boss encounters should have a maximum of four phases.

Companion system
Companions should be actual characters, not just NPCs following the player.

Each important companion should have:
- Their own Gift.
- Personal quests.
- Unique combat abilities.
- Dialogue choices.
- Relationship progression.
- Personal history.
- Interactions with other companions.
Companions can also help players discover new spell combinations.
