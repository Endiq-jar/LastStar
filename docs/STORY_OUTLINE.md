# Last Star — story outline

This is a direction for the story, not a list of completed quests or dungeons.
The playable story currently covers Ilyra's first quests and the first three
Hollow memories; the later acts and ending below are proposed and unimplemented.

## Premise

Long ago the First Silence nearly extinguished the light of Elaris. The Seven
Seals still protect the world, but Hollows are breaking through and the past is
preserved only in scattered memories. People possess Gifts, and a Wander helps
them learn to shape those Gifts using Light.

## Act I — A light remembered

In Ashvale, the player meets Ilyra of the Star Guard and recovers a star sigil.
Ilyra can join the journey and help the player in battle. In Ashvale Hollow, a
Warden guards a fragment: a memory of the people who shared their light when
the Silence came. The player can share its truth with the families or preserve
it for research, changing Ilyra's bond and the spell learned.

## Act II — Pieces of the past

Elaris's settlements hold different parts of a broken account. In Lunareth's
Memory Vault, a record describes the Seals as a shield for the people. At
Suncrest, a dragon guards the memory of a caravan that carried knowledge through
the catastrophe. Twenty-five story dungeons are intended to each reveal one
memory, while other dungeons provide optional challenges and rewards. Three
memories are currently obtainable. Collecting all twenty-five and the finale
are not implemented yet.

## Act III — The Keeper (proposed)

The Keeper believes that silence and control are the only way to prevent the
catastrophe returning. As the memories are assembled, the player discovers
what the Seals protect, why the Hollows are changing, and what the Keeper has
sacrificed to maintain them. Allies and their choices should shape the journey
rather than simply give the player more power.

## Finale — The last star (proposed)

Beneath Aetheris, a vast underground dungeon descends toward the heart of the
Seals. At its end the Keeper, the strongest boss, confronts the player in a
multi-phase battle. The intended ending resolves the story memories and the
fate of the Seals; its exact dialogue, branching consequences, one-kilometre
level and boss are not yet implemented.
