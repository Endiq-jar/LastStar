# Last Star — story outline

This is a direction for the story, not a list of completed quests or dungeons.
The playable story covers Ilyra's quests, 25 Hollow memories and the Last
Seal. Twenty-two of the memory Hollows share a reusable arena and creature
families; they are not bespoke levels. The broader story and companion arcs
remain substantially shorter than the original design brief.

## Premise

Long ago the First Silence nearly extinguished the light of Elaris. The Seven
Seals still protect the world, but Hollows are breaking through and the past is
preserved only in scattered memories. People possess Gifts, and a Wander helps
them learn to shape those Gifts using Light.

## Act I — A light remembered

In Ashvale, the player meets Ilyra of the Star Guard and recovers a star sigil.
Ilyra can join the journey and help the player in battle. In Ashvale Hollow, a
Warden guards a fragment: a memory of the people who shared their light when
the Silence came. The player can share its truth with the families or preserve
it for research, changing Ilyra's bond and the spell learned.

## Act II — Pieces of the past

Elaris's settlements hold different parts of a broken account. In Lunareth's
Memory Vault, a record describes the Seals as a shield for the people. At
Suncrest, a dragon guards the memory of a caravan that carried knowledge through
the catastrophe. Twenty-five story dungeons each reveal one memory. The named encounters share arena templates and creature families;
their one-time memory and currency rewards persist.

## Act III — The Keeper

The Keeper believes that silence and control are the only way to prevent the
catastrophe returning. As the memories are assembled, the player discovers
what the Seals protect, why the Hollows are changing, and what the Keeper has
sacrificed to maintain them. Allies and their choices should shape the journey
rather than simply give the player more power.

## Finale — The last star

Beneath Aetheris, a vast underground dungeon descends toward the heart of the
Seals. At its end the Keeper, the strongest boss, confronts the player in a
multi-phase battle. The Keeper is defeated, the people renew the Seals together and Elaris
remembers its light. The ending is a short journal conclusion rather than a
cinematic or a branching finale. The full narrative and companion arcs remain
unfinished.


### First five named memory Hollows

The First Warning, A Shared Flame, The Guard's Oath, The Crown's Bargain and
The Scholar's Page each have an authored side chamber, two distinct story
echoes and a named guardian. Both echoes and the scouts must be dealt with
before the guardian appears; leaving or dying resets the unfinished encounter.
Their existing memory IDs and one-time rewards are unchanged. These rooms still
share the main arena and creature/attack systems; they are not yet five fully
bespoke multi-room dungeons. The next five — The Wander's Mark, The Tidal Road, The Drowned Bell, A Prism
of Voices and The Broken Lens — now have the same style of authored side
chamber, echoes and named guardian. They too still share the central arena and
combat systems; the remaining Hollows retain their existing layouts.

## Current progression rules

Complete Ilyra's A Light Remembered and A Memory in the Hollow. Each regional
leader's main retrieval quest provides a written dungeon guide in the journal
(no graphical map), with directions to the regional encounters. Defeat all 25
memory dungeons and all 75 other Hollows before entering the Last Seal. Existing
cleared Hollows and a completed ending remain revisitable on older saves. Side
quest rewards now include a potion and weapon, plus existing cosmetics or spell
blueprints; the mason-style side quest grants equippable stat-only armor. These
quests still use a shared retrieval template, and the full narrative remains
unfinished.


### Third batch of memory Hollows

The Frozen Passage, The Winter Promise, The Caravan's Map, The Keeper's Name,
and The Orchard's Roots now have story echoes, side chambers and named bosses.
They still share the central arena and enemy systems; these are expansions, not
finished bespoke dungeons. Fifteen named memory encounters have these additions.

Snow near Frostmere, savanna near Suncrest, taiga west of Frostmere, tidal flats
near Tidefall's existing pond and a crystal-touched region near Glassheaven use
blended terrain palettes. Sparse pines and fantasy crystals stream by chunk;
height, water, save positions and the hand-painted static grass remain unchanged.
The tidal region does not yet have a dynamic tide. These are initial visual
regions, not complete biome-specific ecosystems or questlines.

### Deeper rooms and trials for the fifteen expanded Hollows

Each of the fifteen named memory Hollows now continues beyond the annex into a
second chamber. The depths vary; the second echo is either recovered in order
or unsealed by defeating the guards. A named, telegraphed floor trial guards the
inner room, and each named boss favours one of three attack patterns. Echoes can
be reread after a victory. Save IDs, existing rewards and the Last Seal gate are
unchanged. These improvements still reuse modular rooms, enemy models, and shared
combat and puzzle mechanics; they do not yet constitute fifteen fully bespoke,
individually playtested dungeons.
