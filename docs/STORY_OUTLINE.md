# Last Star — story outline

The playable main narrative follows Ilyra, 25 Hollow memories, the Last Seal
and a return to Ashvale. Its five-act journal uses existing memory encounters as
its 25 objectives, rather than adding 25 separate bespoke quest systems.
Twenty-two of the memory Hollows share modular rooms and creature families;
they are not bespoke levels. The broader side-quest and companion scope remains
shorter than the original design brief.

## Story chronicle update

All 25 existing memory clears now unlock individual written accounts in the
journal, with a hook for each of the 14 settlement leaders. Ilyra comments on
specific recovered memories and the opening establishes the missing-patrol
mystery. Three optional, interactable echoes on the Last Seal descent give the
Keeper and a witness opposing voices. The journal and Story page show an ending
that reflects the player's existing share/study decision; old saves derive the
accounts from their existing clear flags. These are written dialogue and
interactions, not new cinematics, bespoke levels, or an expanded companion arc.

## Regional and finale dialogue

The 14 leaders now have authored discoveries and completion payoffs linked to
their existing quest items. At the end of the Last Seal, players can record the
Keeper's testimony publicly or keep the Keeper's own memory private while the
witnesses retain their accounts. The decision is optional until selected,
persists in existing story saves, changes the written ending and later dialogue,
and does not alter rewards or entry requirements. Older completed saves can
return to the Last Seal to decide. These are narrative consequences, not a new
branch of levels or cinematics.

## Ilyra — The Names We Carry

After resolving the Ashvale memory, players can accept Ilyra's optional personal
quest. They read an interactable memorial in Ashvale, earn Captain Ren's trust
through his existing capital quest and ask about the sealed order, then recover
the existing Memory Vault record. They choose whether to read the names at the
memorial or bring private letters to the families. The player then returns to
the memorial or speaks to Mira to deliver the letters. The decision changes
Ilyra's later dialogue and the written ending; completing either path strengthens
her existing healing support and grants 60 starlight once. Quest stage and choice are saved,
old clears count, and this quest does not gate the main story or finale.

## Premise

Long ago the First Silence nearly extinguished the light of Elaris. The Seven
Seals still protect the world, but Hollows are breaking through and the past is
preserved only in scattered memories. People possess Gifts, and a Wander helps
them learn to shape those Gifts using Light.

## Act I — A light remembered

In Ashvale, the player meets Ilyra of the Star Guard and recovers a star sigil.
Ilyra can join the journey and help the player in battle. In Ashvale Hollow, a
Warden guards a fragment: a memory of the people who shared their light when
the Silence came. The player can share its truth with the families or preserve
it for research, changing Ilyra's bond and the spell learned.

## Act II — Pieces of the past

Elaris's settlements hold different parts of a broken account. In Lunareth's
Memory Vault, a record describes the Seals as a shield for the people. At
Suncrest, a dragon guards the memory of a caravan that carried knowledge through
the catastrophe. Twenty-five story dungeons each reveal one memory. The named encounters share arena templates and creature families;
their one-time memory and currency rewards persist.

## Act III — The Keeper

The Keeper believes that silence and control are the only way to prevent the
catastrophe returning. As the memories are assembled, the player discovers
what the Seals protect, why the Hollows are changing, and what the Keeper has
sacrificed to maintain them. Allies and their choices should shape the journey
rather than simply give the player more power.

## Finale — The last star

Beneath Aetheris, a vast underground dungeon descends toward the heart of the
Seals. At its end the Keeper, the strongest boss, confronts the player in a
multi-phase battle. The Keeper is defeated, the people renew the Seals together and Elaris
remembers its light. The ending is a choice-aware journal conclusion and playable homecoming rather
than a cinematic or a branching level. Broader companion and side-quest arcs
remain unfinished.


### First five named memory Hollows

The First Warning, A Shared Flame, The Guard's Oath, The Crown's Bargain and
The Scholar's Page each have an authored side chamber, two distinct story
echoes and a named guardian. Both echoes and the scouts must be dealt with
before the guardian appears; leaving or dying resets the unfinished encounter.
Their existing memory IDs and one-time rewards are unchanged. These rooms still
share the main arena and creature/attack systems; they are not yet five fully
bespoke multi-room dungeons. The next five — The Wander's Mark, The Tidal Road, The Drowned Bell, A Prism
of Voices and The Broken Lens — now have the same style of authored side
chamber, echoes and named guardian. They too still share the central arena and
combat systems; the remaining Hollows retain their existing layouts.

## Current progression rules

Complete Ilyra's A Light Remembered and A Memory in the Hollow. Each regional
leader's main retrieval quest provides a written dungeon guide in the journal
(no graphical map), with directions to the regional encounters. Defeat all 25
memory dungeons and all 75 other Hollows before entering the Last Seal. Existing
cleared Hollows and a completed ending remain revisitable on older saves. Side
quest rewards now include a potion and weapon, plus existing cosmetics or spell
blueprints; the mason-style side quest grants equippable stat-only armor. These
quests still use a shared retrieval template, and the full narrative remains
unfinished.


### Third batch of memory Hollows

The Frozen Passage, The Winter Promise, The Caravan's Map, The Keeper's Name,
and The Orchard's Roots now have story echoes, side chambers and named bosses.
They still share the central arena and enemy systems; these are expansions, not
finished bespoke dungeons. Fifteen named memory encounters have these additions.

Snow near Frostmere, savanna near Suncrest, taiga west of Frostmere, tidal flats
near Tidefall's existing pond and a crystal-touched region near Glassheaven use
blended terrain palettes. Sparse pines and fantasy crystals stream by chunk;
height, water, save positions and the hand-painted static grass remain unchanged.
The tidal region does not yet have a dynamic tide. These are initial visual
regions, not complete biome-specific ecosystems or questlines.

### Deeper rooms and trials for the fifteen expanded Hollows

Each of the fifteen named memory Hollows now continues beyond the annex into a
second chamber. The depths vary; the second echo is either recovered in order
or unsealed by defeating the guards. A named, telegraphed floor trial guards the
inner room, and each named boss favours one of three attack patterns. Echoes can
be reread after a victory. Save IDs, existing rewards and the Last Seal gate are
unchanged. These improvements still reuse modular rooms, enemy models, and shared
combat and puzzle mechanics; they do not yet constitute fifteen fully bespoke,
individually playtested dungeons.


### Fourth batch of memory Hollows

The Seed of Light, The Fortress Wall, The Shield's Cost, The Twilight Witness,
and The Missing Voices now have authored story echoes, deeper chambers, named
trials and tuned guardians. The same modular environment, enemies and puzzle
rules are reused; none has received a full manual playtest. Twenty named memory
Hollows have been expanded in this way, not twenty fully bespoke dungeons.

### Twenty regional expeditions and fixed difficulty tiers

The first 20 regional Hollow IDs (`optional_000` through `optional_019`) now
have named guardians, authored echoes, deeper modular chambers and two
telegraphed trials. Their IDs and one-time rewards stay unchanged. The first 20
named memory Hollow inner rooms have also been lengthened and given a second
trial. These are expansions of the existing shared systems, not 40 bespoke
levels; the remaining regional Hollows still use the simpler arena template.

The first tier is the twenty named memory Hollows `memory_04` through
`memory_23`, which use normal strength. Once those are cleared, the twenty
regional expeditions `optional_000` through `optional_019` unlock (alongside
the existing story and regional-guide requirements). Their enemies have 20×
health and deal 20× damage, and their floor trials deal 20× damage. No difficulty
setting is required. Previously cleared regional Hollows remain revisitable on
older saves. After those twenty victories, the next twenty regional expeditions unlock at
25× the second tier (500× the first tier). The next twenty regional expeditions
unlock after those, at 15× the third tier (7,500× the first tier).
The next twenty regional expeditions are 2× the fourth tier (15,000× the first).
The Last Seal's defenders and Keeper also use 15,000× strength. The original
100 Hollows remain the finale requirement; five newly added Hollows are optional.
This strength can cause one-hit defeats and has not been balanced in a full
manual playthrough.


### Third difficulty tier: regional Hollows 21–40

The 20 regional encounters `optional_020` through `optional_039` now have named
guardians, story fragments, longer modular inner rooms and three telegraphed
trials. Their enemy health and damage and trial damage are 25× the previous
regional tier (500× the first tier). Their save IDs and one-time rewards are
unchanged. The journal displays both tier prerequisites and their strength.
These encounters still share assets, rooms, enemies and puzzle systems; they
are not fully bespoke. Existing player progression was not redesigned around
these extreme health pools, so practical completion and balance remain untested.


### Fourth tier: regional Hollows 41–60

The existing `optional_040` through `optional_059` now have named guardians,
story fragments, longer modular inner rooms and four telegraphed trials. Enemy
health and damage and trial damage are 15× the preceding tier. They require the
first three tiers' 20 clears each plus the existing story and regional guide.
Existing clears stay revisitable; save IDs and one-time payouts remain intact.
These use shared modular assets and mechanics, not bespoke dungeons. Player
progression is not scaled to these extreme values, so viable completion and
fairness require manual playtesting and balancing.


### Fifth tier: regional Hollows 61–80

The remaining fifteen original regional Hollows (`optional_060`–`optional_074`)
and five new Hollows (`optional_075`–`optional_079`) now form a fifth tier.
They have named guardians and echoes, longer modular rooms and five telegraphed
floor trials. Enemy health and damage and trial damage are 2× the previous tier.
They require the preceding four groups of twenty, plus story and guide access.
Old clears stay revisitable and original IDs and payouts are unchanged. Only the
original 100 Hollows count towards the finale; the five new ones cannot replace
missing originals. There are 105 Hollows in total. Assets and mechanics are shared,
and balance and practical completion at these extreme numbers are untested.


### The final two named memories and connections

The Underground Path and The Last Star now have their own two echoes, deeper
chambers, named guardians and floor trials, using the same modular encounter
systems as the other twenty named memories. Eight journal connections join
specific recovered accounts into a coherent investigation of the Keeper and the
Seals. Each requires both supporting memories, works in any discovery order and
uses existing clear flags, so earlier saves reveal the same connections. Ilyra
also reacts to the final two records before the Last Seal. These additions are
not bespoke levels or cinematics, and the broader companion and side-quest
brief remains unfinished.

Each settlement leader also has a distinct post-Keeper conversation describing
what their community does with the recovered history. These are dialogue
conclusions, not new settlement events or additional side-quest chains.

## A Bell Between Shores

After Neri's existing Tidefall leader quest, players can help design a warning
system. The Drowned Bell and Prism of Voices memories provide evidence, and
Prism's completed leader quest opens a conversation in Glassheaven. Players
choose distributed boat signals or a public harbour station, then return to
Neri to learn Harbor Light, a defensive shield-and-stamina spell. The plan
changes Neri's later dialogue. Existing clears count and quest stage and choice
persist; this does not alter Hollow gates. This is one authored optional quest,
not a replacement for the brief's complete side-quest and companion scope.

## Seven Witnesses

After the Keeper falls, seven optional lights appear in the Last Seal arena.
Each interaction shares a recorded promise from a settlement leader. They can
be heard in any order, survive leaving and re-entering, and add a renewal passage
to the journal ending after all seven are heard. Older completed saves can return
to the arena. This does not gate the existing ending or change boss rewards, and
uses recorded voices rather than claiming that the NPCs physically appear.

## The Wander That Waited

Veya's optional investigation connects the Wander's Mark and Twilight Witness
memories with Rook's Evergloom survey. The player follows Rook's lead to an
interactable lost Wander and decides whether to preserve its anonymous message
publicly or bring it to the families first. The choice persists in Veya's later
dialogue and the ending. Either path teaches Echo Resonance, a short-range
line-of-sight damage and slow spell; bosses resist the slow. Existing clears
count and no new main-story gates are introduced. This is one additional side
quest, not the complete companion or side-quest scope of the original brief.

## Neri as a companion

Completing A Bell Between Shores lets players invite Neri to travel or send her
home. Her existing Tidefall resident is replaced while she follows and restored
when dismissed, retaining the same dialogue and quests. In nearby combat, her
Gift restores Light on a cooldown; when Ilyra is also following, their combined
support additionally grants a small ward. Both characters acknowledge each
other in dialogue. Her recruitment is saved and remains optional. Her model is
still the existing procedural resident and she has no bespoke animations; other
planned companions and relationships are not represented by this addition.

## A Watch for Everyone

After Bryn's Frostmere leader quest, a watch marker starts a real two-attacker
settlement defense. If the player is defeated or leaves the streamed settlement,
the unfinished wave resets and can be signalled again. On victory Bryn asks
whether to organise shared patrols or guided evacuations; the choice changes
later dialogue and the ending. Either resolution teaches Watchfire, a shield and
healing spell. Progress uses new keys in the existing story dictionary and does
not change the 105 Hollow roster or the original finale gate. The encounter uses
the existing scout enemies, not bespoke models or animations.

## Fixed-tier Hollow resonance

Clearing each required group of twenty now earns offensive resonance for the
next eligible Hollow tier: 10×, 200×, 2,000×, then 3,000×. The Last Seal uses
the final value. This is derived from existing clear flags, capped to the
current encounter and removed when leaving or escaping; it does not apply in
the overworld. Enemy health, enemy and trial damage, group requirements, and
the original 100-Hollow finale gate are unchanged. It reduces the otherwise
extreme time needed to defeat late-tier enemies, but their attacks remain
potentially lethal in one hit. These values have not had a complete manual
playthrough or device performance verification.

## Main journey and homecoming

The journal now groups all 25 existing story memories into five authored acts,
with a premise, a distinct objective for every memory and a conclusion when
each act is recovered. Acts suggest a reading order but do not block exploration:
out-of-order and old-save clears count immediately. After the Keeper's memory
is resolved and the seven optional witness lights are heard, players can return
to Ilyra in Ashvale for a playable homecoming and a one-time 150-starlight reward.
The conclusion reflects the opening answer and recorded story decisions, opens
a readable ending card and can be revisited from the Story screen. The
existing ending and original 100-Hollow gate remain unchanged; this is not a
cinematic, and the regional Hollow arenas and many resident quests remain shared.

## Regional leader side stories

Each of the fourteen settlement leaders' existing side quests now has a unique
title, motive, recovered-item discovery, resolution and post-Keeper follow-up.
They use the same physical items, quest IDs, statuses and rewards as before, so
old in-progress and completed saves continue at the appropriate stage. These
are authored narrative layers over existing retrieval quests, not fourteen new
combat encounters or the full set of bespoke side quests in the original brief.

## Veya as a companion

After The Wander That Waited, Veya can travel with the player or return to
Lunareth. Her existing resident is removed while she follows and restored on
dismissal, so her quests remain accessible without a duplicate. Her Gift slows
one nearby scout in line of sight on a cooldown; with Neri following, it also
interrupts the scout. Large guardians resist that support. Dialogue reflects
her personal history and her cooperation with Neri. Recruitment persists in
old-compatible story data. Her appearance and animation still use the shared
resident assets rather than a bespoke character model.

The ending also recalls the completed harbour quest's actual decision: either
boat-to-boat warnings or the accountable harbour station. The decision is read
from the existing saved choice; unfinished harbour stories add no outcome.

## The Last Caravan

After Zara's Suncrest leader quest, a missing medicine caravan leads to Bryn in
Frostmere. Once Bryn's quest is complete, the player searches the packs east of
Frostmere and defeats two scouts; interrupted or unloaded encounters can be
retried. Zara offers two plans: free stocked shelters or paired guides carrying
travellers who cannot pay. Both grant the equippable Caravan hairpin once; Zara,
Bryn and the ending reflect the choice. This uses existing save data without
changing the original Hollow requirements. The encounter uses existing scout models. After the rescue, guide Tala and
traveller Esen appear at the shelter and can be spoken to; Zara acknowledges
each account when heard. These are simple static resident-style models, not
bespoke art or escort AI. Speaking to them is optional, including for older
saves already waiting to report to Zara.

## What the Roots Remember

Orin asks the player to hear three root marks around the Orchard in any order.
Prism can interpret their accounts once the player has recovered the Orchard's
Roots and Seed of Light and completed Prism's existing main quest. Orin then
asks whether to share a cutting with Glassheaven or shelter it in the Orchard.
The resulting sapling is an inspectable, streamed world object in the chosen
settlement; both leaders and the ending acknowledge the decision. Both choices
teach Rootward, a shield that briefly slows nearby scouts in sight. Quest flags
are additive, preserve existing Hollow IDs, and save partial investigation
progress. The small quest objects are procedural and are not bespoke character
models or a new dungeon encounter.
